title: "Probabilistic Refinement Session Types"
desc: >-
  A novel theory of probabilistic refinement session types (PReST) to
  symbolically specify and reason about probabilistic message-passing
  concurrent programs. Its soundness ensures the distributions specified in
  communication protocols are respected at runtime, and refinement types can
  even statically verify parametric distributions such as the uniform
  distribution over {0, ..., k}. Implemented as a type checker in OCaml
  using Z3 and CVC5.
